Garten, grown up

Fluent's bones (layers, acrylic, decelerate motion, clear focus) with an identity only Garten could have.

One icon for state

  • Seed, sprout or bloom: one growth icon next to the title says where a spec, word or rule is. No step-by-step track.
  • The only action that fits that state sits on the same line: “Ask for approval”, “Let it bloom”, “Accept”.
  • Moving on grows the icon in place: stem, leaves, flower, a short pollen burst.

Words and guardrails are definitions

  • Modelled on the term and rule files on main: a word has a body in Markdown plus other names, words to avoid, “not the same as” and see-also. A rule has its id, Always or Never, the binding sentence and a body that says why and how.
  • Each opens as its own page, edited where it is read. Details appear only when they have a value; “Add detail” offers the missing ones.
  • In a spec, a highlighted word shows its definition on hover and opens its page on click. Other names resolve to the same word.
  • Agent proposals carry the sprout icon and wait for Accept, in the list, on the page and on Today.

At 100+ specs, words, rules and documents

  • Overviews never grow with the data. Today shows the five most recent specs per stage; Context the first eight words and documents and four rules per kind. At most three proposals show there, with “N more proposals” for the rest.
  • The section heading opens the full list for that kind; only “N more proposals” stays as an extra link.
  • Full lists have one filter field and a few filters with counts. Groups keep them scannable: specs by stage, words A–Z with a jump row, guardrails by Always and Never, documents by folder. Group headings stick while you scroll.
  • Every section heading opens its full list; the arrow next to it moves on hover. Seed, Sprout and Bloom, Always and Never too.
  • Rows render 60 at a time and load more as you scroll, so 1,000 items stay fast.
  • Lists sort by Recent or A–Z and group by the same: Today, This week, This month, Older. Folder chips narrow words, guardrails and documents. Matches are highlighted.
  • Opening an item from a list and going back returns to the same filter, sort and scroll position.
  • Proposals can be selected (click the box, or x on the keyboard) and accepted or declined together.
  • Today's stack links to “Needs you”, the whole queue as a list: questions, approvals and proposals, with Accept right in the row.
  • Keyboard: / focuses the filter, arrows move through rows, Enter opens, Esc clears the filter.
  • Ctrl K searches everything, aliases and definitions included, a few results per kind.

Agents live in Today

  • Today is the one place to act: agent questions, blocked agents, approvals and proposals share one stack. A blocked spec is a card, because someone has to unblock it.
  • One line under the headline shows who is working and the latest event. It opens Agents: who works now, the full activity, and History (key numbers, specs bloomed over time, implementation, releases).
  • Connecting an agent is a one-time setup: it lives in the avatar menu and in search.

Documents, Glossary, Guardrails

  • Context is split again into three tabs; each tab is the full list with filter, sort and folders. On desktop the top menu already names the tab, so the page starts with content and “Add” sits in the filter row; the phone keeps the title.
  • Documents open with the foundation in one slim row: Vision and Strategy with their first line, live for every agent. The rest of the screen belongs to the documents.

Fewer elements

  • Spec rows show title, id and age only; type and stats moved out of Today.
  • Guardrails are one list with an Always or Never mark, not two boxes.
  • Explanations live in a first-run tour (avatar menu → Take the tour) and in hover tips.
